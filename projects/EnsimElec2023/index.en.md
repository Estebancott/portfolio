## Diving into the Deep End of the Association

In early 2023, in my third year at ENSIM, I pushed open the door of the **ENSIM'elec** association. I wanted to see from the inside how a robot is built from A to Z. For this first year, I wasn't on the main robot yet, but I was lucky enough to be entrusted with missions that turned out to be incredibly formative.

The theme of the season was **"Cherry on the Cake"**. The concept? Autonomous robots had to stack "cakes" and collect "cherries" to score maximum points in 100 seconds. A challenge both technical and strategic!

![Eurobot 2023 Game Table](/projects/EnsimElec2023/vinyle_table_2023.svg "The official playing field of the Eurobot 2023 competition.")

## My Mission #1: The Cherry Basket 🍒

Our first concrete task was to design and build the team's **cherry basket**. A game element that seems simple but is crucial: at the end of the match, every cherry in the basket earns points. It had to be perfectly compliant with the rules (dimensions, weight) and, above all, easily usable by our robot.

### From SolidWorks to Laser Cutting

We started by modeling the basket on **SolidWorks**, thinking of each part for simple assembly and manufacturing by laser cutting in wood.

![Modeling of the basket on SolidWorks](/projects/EnsimElec2023/panier-decoupe-solidworks.png "3D design of the basket, optimized for laser cutting.")

The first prototype was functional, but a problem quickly appeared: **the weight**. We exceeded the limit allowed by the rules! So we had to go back to the design to **make cutouts** on the side faces. It was an excellent optimization exercise: lightening the structure as much as possible without compromising its strength.

![Final basket with cutouts](/projects/EnsimElec2023/panier-final.png "The assembled basket. You can see the cutouts on the sides, added to meet the weight constraint.")

As can be seen in the illustration above, the basket is disguised as a crocodile because that is the association's mascot, named Murphy.

### The Little Extra: An Intelligent Point Counter

To go further, we decided to make our basket "smart" by integrating an **automatic cherry counter**.
-   **The Brain**: An **Arduino Uno** board for logic.
-   **Detection**: An **ultrasonic sensor** placed above the basket detected each cherry falling into it.
-   **Display**: An **LCD screen** displayed the number of cherries live.

![Electronic schematic of the cherry counter](/projects/EnsimElec2023/schema-compteur.png "Electronic schematic of the counting system.")

## My Mission #2: Organizing the Pre-Cup

In parallel, I was given a big responsibility: organizing the school's **Robotics Pre-Cup**. It is a key event that serves as a dress rehearsal before the real French Cup.

It was an intense dive into event project management:
-   **Team Hunting**: I spent weeks contacting robotics clubs throughout the region, via emails, messages on social networks (Instagram, Twitter...), to convince them to participate.
-   **Official Procedures**: I had to liaise with the national organizers of Eurobot so that our event would be "approved", but also with the ENSIM administration to obtain the necessary authorizations, especially to open the event to the public on Saturday.
-   **Communication**: To attract as many people as possible, I participated in the creation and dissemination of a promotional poster that we plastered all over the school and shared online.

![Poster of the Pre-Cup organized at ENSIM](/projects/EnsimElec2023/affiche-précoupe.png "The event poster, a big communication job!")

## The Trigger for What Followed

This first year was a revelation. Between designing a technical object subject to strong constraints and organizing an event from A to Z, I touched on everything: technology, communication, project management, logistics... I loved this overview and the challenge of making things happen. It is this incredibly rich experience that convinced me to run for and take the presidency of the association the following year.
